- Titles are now mapped to primary commercial intent and capped near the 60-character target.
- Descriptions are written for click-through rate and local specificity.
- Static and dynamic pages now use canonical URLs, OpenGraph/Twitter tags, and JSON-LD.
- Category, city, brand, and location pages include long-form content, FAQs, and internal links.

## Backlink strategy

### Targets

- Photography blogs and gear review sites
- Bangalore filmmaker communities
- Wedding photography blogs and vendor directories
- Event production agencies
- Local Bangalore business directories
- College filmmaking clubs and media societies

### Tactics

- Publish “best cameras for…” guides with embed-ready gear comparison tables
- Offer guest posts for wedding photographers and creator blogs
- Create a “Bangalore creator gear guide” asset for link outreach
- Sponsor or support local photo walks, creator meetups, student film festivals, and short-film screenings
- Build relationships with production houses for partner links and case studies
- Pitch location pages and creator kit guides to local city blogs

## Conversion SEO

- Add trust badges near pricing and cart actions
- Add comparison sections for DSLR vs mirrorless, Sony vs Canon, creator kit vs wedding kit
- Launch “popular gear” and “most rented this month” pages
- Add a rental calculator for body + lens + accessories + number of days
- Add a guided recommendation tool:
  - Ask shoot type, brand preference, budget, number of days, and delivery location
  - Recommend 3 kits with direct add-to-cart or WhatsApp inquiry

## Technical SEO checklist

- Keep ISR and caching on all major commercial pages
- Avoid `force-dynamic` on public landing pages unless absolutely required
- Keep sitemap aligned with actual live routes only
- Keep robots.txt blocking admin, cart, login, register, and experimental pages
- Enable optimized image formats and long-lived caching
- Keep schema coverage for LocalBusiness, Organization, CollectionPage, Product, FAQ, Breadcrumb
- Track Core Web Vitals through Search Console and real-user monitoring
